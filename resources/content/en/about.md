## What is Yameen?

Yameen is a web tool that solves a recurring problem: Arabic letters showing disconnected and reversed in apps without Arabic support. You type your text and click your app, and we copy it in a form that shows correctly there. Alongside it: a fix for Arabic copied from PDFs, a text cleaner, and previews of dozens of free Arabic fonts with your own text.

## Why the name?

Yameen (يمين) means "right". Arabic is written from the right, and the arrow in the logo points in the writing direction.

## How it works

All text processing happens in your browser using open-source libraries. Your text is never sent to a server, and we don't use AI or paid services to process it.

## Fonts

The fonts shown on Yameen are free fonts licensed under the SIL Open Font License, and they belong to their designers. Each font is shown with its designer and licence text, with a link to its official source for download.

## Open data

PDF fix uses an Arabic word list derived from FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave (CC BY-SA 4.0) and from wikipedia-word-frequency by Ilya Semenov, built from Arabic Wikipedia.
