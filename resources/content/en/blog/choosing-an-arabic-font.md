## 1. Where will the text appear?

Large headlines can carry fonts with strong character, such as Kufi and decorative styles. Paragraphs and interfaces need a calm font that stays clear at small sizes, usually a sans or a Naskh designed for reading.

## 2. Which style suits the subject?

- **Kufi:** geometric and strong, for logos, headlines and modern identities.
- **Naskh:** traditional and comfortable for long reading, for books, articles and formal text.
- **Sans:** simple and modern, for apps, websites and ads.
- **Handwriting:** such as Ruqaa and Nastaliq, personal in tone but harder to read in paragraphs.

## 3. Does it have the weights you need?

Good design usually needs at least two weights: regular for text and bold for headings. Families with many weights give you more room for hierarchy.

## 4. Does it handle diacritics well?

If your text uses diacritics, as in religious or educational material, pick a font that places them correctly without clashing with letters. Yameen's fonts page lets you filter by this.

## 5. Will it work in your app?

If you will paste text into an app without Arabic support, the font must include connected letter forms. Fonts marked “Works in apps” on Yameen include them.

## Finally: the licence

Every font on Yameen is licensed under the OFL, which allows commercial use in designs, print and websites. Don't sell the font file itself, and keep the licence text if you distribute the font with your project.
