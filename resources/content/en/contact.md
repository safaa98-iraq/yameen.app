## Before you write

- **Text looks reversed in my app:** your app may support Arabic in your setup, so paste the original text without fixing it.
- **Boxes instead of letters:** choose a font marked “Works in apps”.
- **Payment issue:** include the order number from your account page.

## Reporting a fixing error

Send the text you typed, the app name and version, the font name, and a screenshot of what appeared. It helps us fix the issue quickly.
