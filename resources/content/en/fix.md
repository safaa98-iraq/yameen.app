## Why do letters appear disconnected?

Arabic is stored in logical order, and apps that support Arabic choose each letter's shape and lay the line out right to left. Apps that don't show letters separated and in reverse order.

## What the fixer does

1. **Connect letters:** replaces each letter with the form for its position in the word, and merges lam with alef.
2. **Reverse direction:** orders the line visually, keeping English words and numbers left to right.
3. **Remove diacritics:** drops them, because most apps can't place them correctly.
