## Options

- **Remove diacritics:** removes fatha, damma, kasra, sukun, shadda and tanween.
- **Remove tatweel:** removes the kashida character (ـ).
- **Unify alef:** converts أ, إ and آ to ا.
- **Unify ya:** converts alef maqsura (ى) to ya (ي).
- **Digits:** converts Eastern Arabic digits (٠١٢) to Western digits (012) or back.
- **Spaces:** collapses repeated spaces and trims each line.
